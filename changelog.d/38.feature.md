Linux: both speech engines can run on an NVIDIA GPU. The GPU support pack now has a faster-whisper edition (cuBLAS 12, ~600 MB) and a Parakeet edition (CUDA 13 + cuDNN, ~1.3 GB, offered only on an R580+ driver — older drivers are told to update), activated by an `RTLD_LOCAL` preload. Cleanup now uses the Vulkan `llama-cpp-python` wheel on Linux, and the Recommended chip names Parakeet on NVIDIA with a CUDA-13 driver or on a 4-core, 8 GB CPU.
